Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Project Tech4Dev ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information submitted through the DPDP Readiness Navigator tool ("Tool").

1. What we collect

When you complete the assessment, we collect:

  • Organisation name and size
  • Contact name and email address
  • Sector(s) of work and beneficiary description
  • Your answers to the readiness questionnaire

When you submit a consultation request, we additionally collect your message and service interest.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate your personalised DPDP readiness report and email it to you
  • To provide sector-level aggregate insights across participating organisations (no individual data is published)
  • To respond to consultation requests

We use Anthropic's Claude API to generate report content. The API receives your sector, organisation size, beneficiary description, section scores, and gap question text only — your organisation name, contact name, and email are never sent to any external AI service.

3. Data storage and security

Assessment data is stored on servers located in India. We apply access controls and encryption in transit. Only authorised Tech4Dev staff can access individual assessment records.

4. Data retention

We retain assessment records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet our legal obligations. You may request deletion of your record at any time by contacting us.

5. Third-party services

We use Google Fonts for typography (loaded from Google's CDN). No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are used. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties except as described above.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to access, correct, or request erasure of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at dpdp@projecttech4dev.org.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
