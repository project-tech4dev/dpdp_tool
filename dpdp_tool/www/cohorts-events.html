Cohorts

Three-month cohorts for NGOs

NGOs must be fully compliant with the DPDP Act by May 2027, and many are still working out how to protect the data of the people they serve. Each three-month cohort helps a small group of NGOs build sustainable systems and compliant legal and organisational practices for responsible data governance. The programme combines structured learning, peer exchange, expert advisory and hands-on implementation support. Mentors from Tech4Dev's Fractional CxO (fCxO) team and advisors from Pacta help each organisation understand its obligations and build a clear roadmap, then stay with it through group clinics and one-to-one advisory, while the implementation stays with the organisation. The first cohort brought seven NGOs together in Bangalore in October.

STAGE 1 · MEASURE
Kick-off and self-assessment
A kick-off call, then a detailed self-assessment and a baseline of understanding of the Act before the in-person workshop.
STAGE 2 · ANALYSE AND PLAN
Two-day in-person workshop
Six sessions on the Act, with group activities and individual work on each organisation's own situation. Each participant plans one to five projects.
STAGE 3 · IMPROVE
Eight to ten weeks of virtual work
Group Q&A clinics, one-to-one advisory sessions, and evidence of completed assignments, ending with a closing call.
AFTER THE PROGRAMME
Check-ins
Group check-in calls one month and three months after the closing call.
