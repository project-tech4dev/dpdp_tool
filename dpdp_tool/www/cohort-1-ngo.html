Cohort 1 for NGOs: how the programme runs.

A three-month hybrid programme for seven NGOs, from a kick-off in September 2026 to check-ins in March 2027.

Co-delivered with
The programme

The aim is to build sustainable systems and compliant legal and organisational practices for responsible data governance. They measure where they stand, plan their changes at an in-person workshop, then put the plan into practice with group clinics and one-to-one advisory, while the implementation stays with each organisation. Mentors from Tech4Dev's Fractional CxO (fCxO) team and advisors from Pacta help each organisation understand its obligations and build a clear roadmap. Each NGO takes its own path: some follow their roadmap step by step, some close the most manageable gaps first, and others start with staff awareness and leadership buy-in.

Four stages over three months

STAGE 1 · MEASURE · 18 SEP 2026
Kick-off and self-assessment
A kick-off call, then a detailed self-assessment and a baseline of understanding of the Act before the in-person workshop.
STAGE 2 · ANALYSE AND PLAN · 5 TO 6 OCT 2026
Two-day in-person workshop, Bangalore
Six sessions on the Act, with group activities and individual work on each organisation's own situation. Each participant plans one to five projects.
STAGE 3 · IMPROVE · 16 OCT TO 16 DEC 2026
Ten weeks of virtual work
Group Q&A clinics, ten one-to-one advisory sessions, and evidence of completed assignments. The closing call is on 18 Dec 2026.
AFTER THE PROGRAMME · 15 JAN AND 12 MAR 2027
Check-ins
Group check-in calls one month and three months after the closing call.

Six sessions over two days

SESSION 1
DPDP 101
What the Act covers, its key terms and roles, and what it means for NGOs.
SESSION 2
Data collection and consent
Notices, valid and recorded consent, children's data, and collecting only what you need.
SESSION 3
Storage and security
Knowing what data you hold, who can access it, how long you keep it, and how it is protected.
SESSION 4
Data use and sharing
Using data only for its stated purpose, and sharing it safely with partners and vendors.
SESSION 5
Rights of individuals
Requests to access, correct or erase data, and grievance redressal.
SESSION 6
Governance and processes
Who owns data protection, written policy, staff training, and breach response.
